Account help

Teachers can sign in with Kakao (first login creates their account). Use Edit to set a recognizable name. Grant calendar access via Groups or Calendars tab.

Deactivate blocks sign-in and signs them out. Delete permanently (deactivated accounts only) cannot be undone.
